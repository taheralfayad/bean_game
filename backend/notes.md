- card dealing
  - 3-5 player variant:
    - all players get 5 cards

  - 6-7 player variant:
    - player left of dealer gets 3 cards, second player gets 4, third player gets 5, everyone else gets 6

- cards not present in each variant:
  - 3 player variant:
    - keep all cards
  - 4-5 player variant:
    - remove coffee beans
  - 6-7 player variant:
    - remove cocoa beans and garden beans

- drawing mechanism:
  - 3-5 player variant:
    - each player draws 3 cards
  - 6-7 player variant:
    - each player draws 4
